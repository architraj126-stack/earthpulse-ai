# Scientific Glossary: Earth Science, Remote Sensing & Statistical Analysis

### Albedo ($\alpha$)
The fraction of incoming solar electromagnetic radiation reflected diffusely by a surface back into space, ranging from 0.0 (total absorption) to 1.0 (total reflection). Fresh snow has an albedo of 0.8–0.9, while open asphalt has an albedo of 0.05–0.15.

### Anomaly
The deviation of an observed environmental value from its long-term baseline climatological mean over a defined normal period (typically 30 years).

### Autocorrelation (Serial Correlation)
The correlation between values of the same variable at successive time lags (e.g., today's temperature correlated with yesterday's temperature). Positive serial correlation can lead to false-positive significance in classical trend tests if unadjusted.

### Bowen Ratio ($\beta$)
The ratio of sensible heat flux ($H$) to latent heat flux ($\lambda E$). High in arid urban areas ($\beta > 2.0$); low in humid vegetative biomes ($\beta < 0.5$).

### Climatological Normal
A 30-year average of a weather variable established by the WMO to characterize baseline climate conditions (currently 1991–2020).

### Confidence Interval (CI)
A range of values around an estimate that is expected to contain the true population parameter with a specified probability (e.g., 95%).

### Kendall's Tau ($\tau$)
A non-parametric statistic measuring the ordinal association and concordance between two variables. Bounded between -1.0 (perfect negative monotonic relationship) and +1.0 (perfect positive monotonic relationship).

### Land Surface Temperature (LST)
The thermodynamic radiant skin temperature of the land surface as measured from space by thermal infrared radiometers.

### Mann-Kendall Test
A non-parametric, distribution-free statistical hypothesis test used to detect whether a time series exhibits a monotonic upward or downward trend over time.

### MERRA-2
Modern-Era Retrospective analysis for Research and Applications, Version 2; NASA's global atmospheric reanalysis combining satellite observations with GEOS-5 atmospheric physics.

### NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index)
A satellite-derived remote sensing index calculated from near-infrared and red reflectances, indicating the density and photosynthetic vigor of live green vegetation.

### P-Value
The probability under the null hypothesis of observing a test statistic as extreme as, or more extreme than, the observed value. At $\alpha = 0.05$, a p-value $< 0.05$ is deemed statistically significant.

### Relative Humidity (RH)
The ratio of actual water vapor pressure to the saturation water vapor pressure at the current air temperature, expressed as a percentage.

### Sen's Slope Estimator
The median of all pairwise slopes across a time series, providing a robust, outlier-resistant estimate of the annual rate of change.

### Urban Heat Island (UHI)
A metropolitan area that is significantly warmer than surrounding rural areas due to human activities, heat-absorbing building materials, and lack of vegetation.
