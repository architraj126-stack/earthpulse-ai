# IPCC Sixth Assessment Report (AR6) - The Physical Science Basis

## Overview
The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) AR6 Working Group I report provides the definitive consensus synthesis of climate change observations, attribution, physical processes, and future projections.

## Core Findings on Planetary Warming & Observed Trends
1. **Unprecedented Rate of Warming**:
   - Global surface temperature has increased faster since 1970 than in any other 50-year period over at least the last 2000 years.
   - Each of the last four decades has been successively warmer than any decade that preceded it since 1850.
2. **Hydrological Cycle Acceleration**:
   - Evidence of observed changes in extremes such as heavy precipitation, agricultural and ecological droughts, and tropical cyclones has strengthened since AR5.
   - For every 1°C of atmospheric warming, the Clausius-Clapeyron relation dictates that atmospheric moisture holding capacity increases by approximately 7%.
3. **Detection and Attribution Framework**:
   - Statistical trend detection alone indicates whether an observation differs from zero or baseline randomness.
   - Attribution evaluates whether anthropogenic greenhouse gas forcing, natural volcanic/solar forcings, or internal climate variability explains the detected change using coupled multi-model ensembles (CMIP6).

## Confidence Language Framework
The IPCC standardizes certainty using calibrated qualitative language:
- **Virtually certain**: 99–100% probability
- **Very likely**: 90–100% probability
- **Likely**: 66–100% probability
- **About as likely as not**: 33–66% probability
- **Unlikely**: 0–33% probability
- **Very unlikely**: 0–10% probability
